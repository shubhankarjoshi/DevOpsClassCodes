What We Offer

Complete Eye Care Under One Roof

From routine screenings to advanced surgery, our services are designed around accurate diagnosis and lasting results.

Comprehensive Eye Examination

Full vision and eye-health check-up covering refraction, eye pressure, retina and overall screening.

Cataract Surgery

Painless, stitch-less micro-incision phacoemulsification with a choice of premium intraocular lenses (IOLs).

LASIK & Refractive Surgery

Bladeless, quick-recovery laser vision correction for myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism.

Glaucoma Diagnosis & Management

Eye pressure testing, visual field analysis and long-term treatment to protect the optic nerve.

Pediatric Eye Care & Squint Correction

Vision screening, glasses prescriptions and squint (strabismus) treatment for children.

Contact Lens Clinic

Precise fitting and trial of soft, toric and specialty contact lenses.

Dry Eye Treatment

Advanced evaluation and therapy for chronic dryness, irritation and discomfort.

Diabetic Retinopathy Screening

Retinal examination and laser/injection therapy for diabetes-related eye damage.

Retina Clinic

Diagnosis and treatment of retinal detachment, macular disorders and related conditions.

Oculoplasty & Cosmetic Eye Care

Eyelid surgery and reconstructive procedures performed with precision and care.

Low Vision Aids

Assessment and prescription of aids to maximise remaining vision for daily life.

Eye Emergency Care

Prompt evaluation and treatment for injuries, infections and sudden vision changes.
