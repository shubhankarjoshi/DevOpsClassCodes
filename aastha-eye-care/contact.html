FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need an appointment for a routine eye check-up?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking an appointment helps us minimise your waiting time and ensures the right doctor is available.

Do you accept health insurance for surgeries?

We assist with cashless and reimbursement claims for most major insurance providers. Please bring your policy details during consultation.

How long does cataract surgery take?

Modern phacoemulsification cataract surgery typically takes 15–20 minutes per eye, with most patients going home the same day.

Do you treat children's eye problems?

Yes — our Paediatric Eye Services cover vision screening and treatment for children of all ages.
