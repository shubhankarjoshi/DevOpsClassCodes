Eye Conditions

Accurate Diagnosis, Effective Treatment

We evaluate and manage a wide spectrum of eye conditions affecting patients of every age group.

Cataract

Clouding of the eye's natural lens leading to blurred vision, glare and difficulty seeing at night — treated with modern phaco surgery.

Glaucoma

A condition of raised eye pressure that can silently damage the optic nerve — early detection is key to preventing vision loss.

Refractive Errors

Myopia (near-sightedness), hyperopia (far-sightedness), astigmatism and presbyopia — corrected with glasses, lenses or LASIK.

Dry Eye Syndrome

Persistent burning, irritation, redness or a gritty sensation caused by insufficient or poor-quality tears.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Damage to retinal blood vessels caused by long-term diabetes — requires regular screening and timely treatment.

Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)

Inflammation or infection of the conjunctiva causing redness, discharge and irritation — treated promptly to prevent spread.

Retinal Detachment & Disorders

Serious conditions affecting the retina that need urgent evaluation and specialised surgical or laser treatment.

Squint (Strabismus) & Lazy Eye

Misalignment of the eyes or reduced vision development in children — corrected with therapy, glasses or surgery.

Corneal Ulcers & Infections

Painful, sight-threatening infections of the cornea that need urgent diagnosis and targeted treatment.

Macular Degeneration

Age-related deterioration of the central retina affecting sharp, detailed vision — monitored and managed long-term.

Uveitis

Inflammation inside the eye causing pain, redness and blurred vision — managed with prompt anti-inflammatory treatment.

Computer Vision Syndrome

Digital eye strain from prolonged screen use — causing dryness, headaches and blurred vision, managed with tailored care.
