Eye Conditions

Accurate Diagnosis, Effective Treatment

We evaluate and manage a wide spectrum of eye conditions affecting patients of every age group.

Cataract

Clouding of the eye's natural lens leading to blurred vision, glare and difficulty seeing at night — treated with modern phaco surgery.

Glaucoma

A condition of raised eye pressure that can silently damage the optic nerve — early detection is key to preventing vision loss.

Refractive Errors

Myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism and presbyopia — assessed by computerised eye test and corrected with glasses or contact lenses.

Corneal Disorders

Conditions of the cornea evaluated and treated through our dedicated Cornea and Contact Lens Clinic.

Diabetic Retinopathy & Retinal Disease

Damage to retinal blood vessels from long-term diabetes and other retinal disease, managed with intravitreal injections and laser treatment.

Neuro-Ophthalmic Disorders

Vision problems linked to the nervous system, diagnosed and managed through our Neuro-Ophthalmology service.

Paediatric Eye Conditions

Vision problems in children, evaluated and treated through our dedicated Paediatric Eye Services.

Blocked Tear Duct

Nasolacrimal duct obstruction causing watery eyes, corrected with DCT/DCR surgery.
