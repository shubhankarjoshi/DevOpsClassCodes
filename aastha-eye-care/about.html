Our Story

Dedicated to Protecting the Vision of Ahilyanagar

Aastha Eye Care was founded with a simple purpose — to make quality, affordable eye care accessible to every family in Ahilyanagar (Ahmednagar). What began as a small clinic has grown into a trusted destination for comprehensive eye examinations, advanced surgical care, and long-term management of eye conditions.

Our team combines modern diagnostic technology with a warm, patient-first approach, ensuring every visit — from a routine check-up to a surgical procedure — feels comfortable, transparent and reassuring.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, affordable and compassionate eye care to every patient who walks through our doors.

Our Vision

To be Ahilyanagar's most trusted eye care centre, known for clinical excellence and genuine patient care.

Our Values

Honesty in diagnosis, transparency in pricing, and respect for every patient's time and trust.
