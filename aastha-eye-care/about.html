Our Story

Dedicated to Protecting the Vision of Ahmednagar

With over 10 years of experience, Aastha Eye Care is run by Dr. Ganesh Sarda and Dr. Chaitrali Somani-Sarda from our clinic on Savedi Road, Ahmednagar, offering comprehensive eye examinations, advanced surgical care, and long-term management of eye conditions for the whole community.

Our team combines modern diagnostic technology with a warm, patient-first approach, ensuring every visit — from a routine check-up to a surgical procedure — feels comfortable, transparent and reassuring.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, affordable and compassionate eye care to every patient who walks through our doors.

Our Vision

To be Ahmednagar's most trusted eye care centre, known for clinical excellence and genuine patient care.

Our Values

Honesty in diagnosis, transparency in pricing, and respect for every patient's time and trust.
